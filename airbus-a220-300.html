Airbus A220-300

The Airbus A220-300 is a narrow-body jet airliner designed for short to medium-haul flights. It features no safety features, engines produced in the late 1990s and a cramped interior layout that allows for a higher passenger capacity. The A220-300 is known for its loud and bumpy ride, making it a popular choice for regional routes.
